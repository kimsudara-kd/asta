# ADR-0010: Durable work queue, effectively-once posting, replay recovery, reconciliation

Status: accepted (M2)

**Queue.** PostgreSQL table `durable_jobs` (no Redis/Kafka/Kubernetes: one host, one database, tens of jobs per hour per session; no concrete limitation requires more). Idempotent creation by unique `job_key`; leasing with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, lease expiry (crashed worker ⇒ job claimable again), heartbeat/ownership checks on complete/fail, bounded retries with exponential backoff (5 s, cap 300 s), failure classes TRANSIENT / PERMANENT, terminal `DEAD` rows that stay visible and open a CRITICAL incident, poison-pill protection (a job whose retry budget is spent is dead-lettered instead of re-leased), cancellation of a session's pending work, worker heartbeats in `worker_leases`, graceful drain. STEP jobs of one session are strictly serialised in creation order (NOT EXISTS earlier unfinished STEP of the same session).
**Delivery vs effects.** Delivery is *at least once*. Financial effects are *effectively once*: a step is ONE transaction that (1) locks the session row, (2) advances `last_step_seq` with a compare-and-set, (3) inserts the step input and every order event / fill / ledger entry / balance projection / equity snapshot / risk event / signal / checkpoint, (4) completes the job. A redelivered step finds the cursor advanced ⇒ `StepAlreadyPosted` and nothing is written; unique keys `(session, account, fill_id)`, `(session, account, ledger seq)`, `(session, step_seq)`, `(session, step_ts)` are the second line of defence. Exactly-once *external* delivery is not claimed.
**Recovery by deterministic replay.** The only inputs of the simulation are the frozen session config (hashed, immutable) and the exact bars/flags consumed at each step (`paper_step_inputs`, integrity-hashed). After any interruption the runtime is rebuilt by replaying the stored inputs and every rebuilt checkpoint digest is compared with the durable one; a mismatch, a gap in the step sequence, a tampered input, a missing checkpoint or a wrong cursor raises `ReplayMismatch` ⇒ the job is dead-lettered permanently and the session is **not** resumed (fail-closed). Replay never fabricates anything: it re-derives only what the identical recorded inputs imply, and verifies it against the persisted rows.
**Reconciliation (durable rows only).** Ledger sums per account/asset vs the balance projection; an independent fills-derived balance vs the ledger; last equity snapshot vs balances × marks; checkpoint fill count vs stored fills; no negative/over-reserved balances. Run after every step that posted fills, every 12 steps, every 15 minutes (RECONCILE job), at resume, and before a daily report. Any discrepancy opens a CRITICAL incident and moves the session to FAILED (suspended) — history is never edited to "repair" it.
**Data interruption.** Missing/incomplete 1-minute buckets ⇒ no session bar ⇒ symbol blocked from new entries (`DATA_GAP`); a feed that is not CONNECTED/fresh ⇒ `STALE_DATA` block; recovery is automatic only when contiguous complete bars return; incidents are opened/resolved per market with dedupe keys.
