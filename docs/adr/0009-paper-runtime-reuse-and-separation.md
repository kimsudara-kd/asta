# ADR-0009: Paper-trading runtime — reuse of the M1 engine, accounts, global risk, physical separation

Status: accepted (M2)

**One simulation, not two.** The paper runtime drives the *same* `research.sim.Engine` (ledger, conservative candle-only fill model, risk rules, strategies) as M1. `Engine.run()` became a thin loop over `begin / settle / decide`; `step()` is the M1 path (settle → own risk → decide). The refactor is behaviour-preserving: the research tests pass unchanged and the seven full-period real-data runs reproduce their original M1 result checksums bit-for-bit after the change. There is no second ledger, no second fee model and no second interpretation of P&L.
**Accounts.** One engine per (venue, strategy) slice of that venue's virtual wallet (500/500 USDT by default; remainder of the rounded split goes to the first slice; sums are exact). Portfolio equity = Σ active accounts + virtual cash of venues without a verified feed (never reallocated). Two **shadow benchmark accounts** (reference-policy-constrained hold 3×100 and unconstrained passive hold 3×333, each on a separate virtual 1,000 USDT) and an analytic all-cash line are reported next to the portfolio; they never count toward portfolio equity or global risk, and the report shows both benchmarks (never a "fair buy-and-hold" claim from a constrained one).
**Global risk.** One shared `RiskEngine` judged on *global* equity once per step (phase 2 between `settle` and `decide`), plus optional `PortfolioHooks` that add other accounts' exposure to the per-asset (10 %) and total (30 %) caps and block entries on stale/gapped markets. Default `None` hooks ⇒ unchanged M1 behaviour.
**Halt semantics (documented policy).** A halt blocks risk-increasing orders and cancels open buys; sells/exits remain allowed; resting protective sells stay. Daily-loss trip does **not** reset at midnight (`auto_reset_daily=False`); an operator `rearm_daily` is allowed only on a later UTC day and is recorded in the step inputs so replay agrees; a drawdown halt is latched for the whole session (stop the session). M1 keeps the ADR-0007 auto re-arm for backtests.
**Muted steps.** A paused, halted-for-entries or *late* step (catching up after downtime, beyond `max(3 × stale_after, 300 s)`) still consults the strategy with `is_warmup=True` so its indicator state stays aligned with the market, but every proposed order is rejected as WARMUP, cancels are ignored and nothing is persisted as a signal. No decision is ever made from a stale bar.
**Physical separation.** `asta.paper` may not import `asta.exchanges`, any HTTP/WebSocket/process library, nor credential/signing vocabulary; `asta.exchanges` never imports `asta.paper` (AST tests). Only the composition root `asta.paper_service` wires the existing egress-checked *public* adapters to the collector. `ExecutionGateway` stays default-DENY, `Environment` has no LIVE, the egress allow-list still denies private/trading/business paths. Paper orders are rows in `paper_*` tables produced by the offline simulator.
**Strategy policy.** All strategies are marked `deployment_candidate: false` ("no validated edge (M1)") in every config, API response and report; parameters and registry versions are recorded; rejected signals are stored with reason codes; signals are separate from simulated fills.
